> The ROADMAP goal is not in `As a / I want to / so that` form. No user story has been invented.

**This plan's slice:** the corridor becomes the surreal place it was designed to be — reflections that show what is not built, architecture that assembles as you approach, and an ending — and its cost is measured rather than assumed.

three@0.186.0 facts verified from source — do not re-derive:
  - `WebGLRenderer.projectObject` filters **lights** by camera layers exactly as it filters meshes,
    so a layer-0 light is not collected when rendering with a layer-2 camera.
  - `Reflector.getReflectionCamera(camera)` lazily does `camera.clone()`, and `Object3D.copy`
    executes `this.layers.mask = source.layers.mask`, so the clone starts on layer 0 and there is no
    option to configure it. Force the camera to exist, then override.
  - `Reflector`'s options expose only `textureWidth`, `textureHeight`, `clipBias`, `shader`,
    `multisample` and `color`; it constructs the `WebGLRenderTarget` internally, so
    `depthBuffer: true` / `generateMipmaps: false` are satisfied by render-target defaults and are
    relied upon rather than asserted through the API.
  - `Reflector` costs about 1.3 KB gzipped over the no-reflection bundle and already implements the
    Lengyel oblique near-plane clipping a hand-roll gets wrong. Do not fork it.
</interfaces>

<decisions>
- **Phones take the primary reflection path deliberately.** The cheap-fallback trigger is
  `viewportWidth × dpr < 700`; a 390 px phone at dpr 3 evaluates to 1170 and does not trip it. The
  intended mobile baseline is the full reflection with the render target capped at 1024 px and the
  shadow map at 512², which is why §C.2 halves the shadow map below 768 px rather than disabling
  reflections. Slow devices are caught by the measured quality ladder, never by a screen-size guess.
- **`multisample: 0` when device pixel ratio exceeds 1.5.** MSAA on a 1024² render target is the
  phone's single biggest cost, and `Reflector` does not expose the render-target flags any other way.
- **No frame-rate number is produced by this plan.** Every measurement recorded is a byte count, a
  pixel dimension or an object count. Phase 3 owns PERF-01 and the physical-device evidence.
</decisions>
