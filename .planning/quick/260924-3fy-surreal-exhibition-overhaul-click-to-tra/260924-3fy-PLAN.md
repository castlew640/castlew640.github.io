# Quick Task 260924-3fy: Surreal exhibition overhaul

## Owner request (2026-09-24, verbatim intent)

"It just feels clunky … the [glass/vibe] doesn't really sit well. We're not able to view it … I only wanted it to be a forward scroll, but clickability might be something we need to add. We want more details, melting clocks, fun stuff … set you loose, go crazy, have fun."

## Observed problems (screenshots of the built site, 1440×900 and iPhone 13)

1. 3D mode stacks opaque paper slabs from the catalogue over the canvas (hero, "↓" band, "Projects" band, split About/Resume boxes). They slide across the scene and hide it.
2. Exhibits hang overhead in stone portals; the screenshot is small and far away. There is no way to look at the evidence closely without leaving for the case-study page.
3. The ivory/ink palette reads as an architectural drawing, not a Dalí dream. No clocks, no playfulness, nothing reacts to the pointer.
4. Scroll maps linearly to the camera, so a mouse wheel produces steppy motion and the camera never settles on an exhibit.
5. The contact beer glass (D-22) is 44 px, floats mid-heading, and reads as a waste bin.
6. Phone still view: the view toggle overlaps the header copy; the arrow boxes and endpoint status float over body text.

## Decisions (Claude's discretion, granted by "set you loose")

- **D-Q1 World.** Replace the ivory corridor with a golden-hour Dalí plain: gradient sky with low sun, ochre desert, checkerboard causeway, distant cliffs and sea, long shadows, sky-lit environment map. Ivory paper remains the text surface and brand.
- **D-Q2 Exhibits.** Each project is a large gilded frame on an easel, crutches, or spindly legs, facing the approaching visitor, with a melting clock draped over a corner. Travel settles in front of each frame so its evidence fills the free part of the screen.
- **D-Q3 Motifs.** Melting clocks that show the visitor's real time (dead-tree block at the entrance, frames, sofa); orange pocket watch with ants; long-legged elephants on the horizon; Mae West lips sofa (About); chest of drawers holding the resume; lobster telephone and a pint glass (Contact); a floating rock castle (Magritte nod to "Castle") as the destination beacon; an egg where new work hatches.
- **D-Q4 Clickability.** Tap/click a distant exhibit or landmark to travel to it; tap the exhibit you are standing at to open an in-place viewer; tap props for small reactions (clocks rewind time, ants scatter, elephants trumpet, egg wobbles, phone rings); a clickable ground arrow leads onward. Mouse hover shows a label and pointer cursor. Every action keeps an equivalent HTML control.
- **D-Q5 Viewer (implements v2 DETAIL-01).** Native `<dialog>` per project with every screenshot in a native scroll-snap carousel, captions, contract summary, and case-study/live/repository links. Opening pushes a history entry so Back closes it; closing returns focus. Works in still and 3D views.
- **D-Q6 Placards.** In 3D, each stop's HTML becomes one fixed museum placard (bottom-left desktop, bottom sheet phone) that crossfades with the current stop; focus inside another stop travels there. The canvas stays unobstructed.
- **D-Q7 Motion feel.** Native scroll remains the single travel authority. The camera follows a critically damped copy of the scroll parameter with dwell zones around stops. Ambient animation runs only while the scene is visible and pauses when the tab is hidden, the scene is offscreen, the viewer is open, or the visitor has been idle for a minute. Reduced motion and the still view remain completely motion-free (ACCESS-04).
- **D-Q8 Glass (D-22 kept).** Redraw as a larger nonic pint that sits on a drawn coaster at the heading baseline; filling pours amber with rising bubbles and a foam head. Still no payment/booking meaning.
- **D-Q9 Still view.** Replace architectural figures with Dalí-motif illustrations (melting clock/tree block, lips sofa, drawers/phone/castle); add "View exhibit" to open the same viewer; move the view toggle into the header; phone controls become a solid bottom bar. D-15 (phones default to still) is kept.
- **D-Q10 Accuracy.** Update the portfolio case study, README and runbook wherever they describe superseded behaviour (demand rendering, reflection, winding portals). Recapture its evidence images from the new build. No new claims about the client work.
